About the Club

Building the motocross community in Bangladesh.

Bangladesh Motocross Club & Academy is operated from Chittagong. The club brings together riders, enthusiasts and motorsports community members around motocross practice, racing, rider development and safe riding.

🏁

Racing

Competitive riding, practice sessions and participation in motorsports events.

🏍️

Academy

Skill development for riders, from motorcycle control and cornering to track riding.

🛡️

Safety

Protective gear, track discipline and responsible riding are central to training.

Leadership

Md. Mahabubur Rahman Hafiz

Director • Champion Racer

Md. Mahabubur Rahman Hafiz is the Director of Bangladesh Motocross Club & Academy and is described by the club as a champion racer. From Chittagong, he is involved in racing and rider development.
